Simple & Safe Workflow

Voluntary Blood Donation Process

A safe 3-step voluntary donation process conducted by healthcare professionals.

STEP 01

1. Donor Registration

Register your profile, blood group, age eligibility, and contact details in seconds.

STEP 02

2. Health Screening & Donation

Quick 10-minute safe collection conducted using single-use sterile medical equipment.

STEP 03

3. Save Up To 3 Lives

Your single voluntary unit is tested, processed, and dispatched to hospital emergency units.
