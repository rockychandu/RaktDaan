Safety Protocols

Blood Donation Safety Guidelines

National medical standards for voluntary blood collection safety.

Medical Screening Protocols

Before every donation, blood bank medical professionals verify donor temperature, blood pressure, pulse rate, and hemoglobin levels (minimum 12.5 g/dL).

Single-Use Sterile Equipment

Every blood collection bag and needle kit is factory sealed, single-use, sterile, and opened exclusively in front of the donor. Needle re-use is strictly impossible.

Post-Donation Rest & Monitoring

Donors rest in observation for 10-15 minutes under medical supervision with refreshments before departure.
