Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Information regarding technical cookies and local storage usage on RaktDaan.

1. Essential Authentication Cookies

RaktDaan utilizes essential local storage keys (auth_token) to preserve your authenticated donor/admin session state across page navigation.

2. UI Preference Storage

Dark mode preference (raktdaan_theme) is stored locally on your device to maintain your preferred visual contrast settings. No advertising or tracking cookies are utilized.
