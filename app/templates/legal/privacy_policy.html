Privacy Policy & Data Security

How RaktDaan protects donor personal data and health records under digital data protection laws.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We collect donor names, contact numbers, email addresses, blood groups, and city locations exclusively for voluntary donor registration and emergency medical matching.

2. Security & Encryption

All user passwords are hashed using bcrypt with custom salt rounds. Sessions are authenticated via JWT token pairs governed by Member 1 security infrastructure.
