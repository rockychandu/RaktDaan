Donor Health

Donor Preparation & Nutrition Guidelines

What to eat, drink, and prepare 24 hours prior to donating blood.

Do's Before Donation

  • ✅ Drink plenty of water or fruit juices (500ml 2 hours before donation).
  • ✅ Eat a healthy, iron-rich meal (spinach, beans, whole grains).
  • ✅ Get a full night's sleep (7-8 hours) prior to donation day.

Don'ts Before Donation

  • ❌ Avoid fatty foods (fries, burgers) as fats interfere with blood testing.
  • ❌ Avoid alcohol consumption for at least 24 hours prior to donation.
  • ❌ Avoid strenuous physical exercise immediately before donating.
