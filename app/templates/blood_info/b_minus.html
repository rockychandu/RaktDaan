Rare Blood Type Profile

Type B Negative (B-) Blood Group

B Negative is an extremely rare blood group in India, present in less than 2% of registered donors.

Transfusion Compatibility

B- blood carries B antigens without the Rh protein.

Can Donate Red Cells To:
B+ B- AB+ AB-
Can Receive Red Cells From:
B- O-

Critical Need

Because B- inventory is scarce, voluntary B- donors are encouraged to register for direct emergency SMS alerts on RaktDaan.
