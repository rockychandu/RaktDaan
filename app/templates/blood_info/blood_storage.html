Blood Component Storage & Lifespan

How blood components are separated and preserved for clinical emergency use.

🔴

Red Blood Cells (RBCs)

Lifespan: Up to 42 Days

Stored in cold refrigeration (2°C–6°C). Used for trauma patients, surgical blood loss, and chronic anemia.

🟡

Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP)

Lifespan: Up to 1 Year

Stored at ultra-low freezing temperatures (-30°C). Used for burn victims, shock, and clotting factor deficiencies.

⚪

Platelets

Lifespan: 5 Days Max

Stored at room temperature (20°C–24°C) with continuous agitation. Critical for Dengue recovery and cancer chemotherapy.
