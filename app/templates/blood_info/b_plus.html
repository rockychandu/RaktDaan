Blood Group Profile

Type B Positive (B+) Blood Group

B+ is the single most common blood group in India, accounting for over 32% of the donor population.

Transfusion Compatibility

Individuals with B+ blood have B antigens on red blood cells and Rh factor protein present.

Can Donate Blood To:
B+ AB+
Can Receive Blood From:
B+ B- O+ O-

Key Facts & Importance

  • 🩸 Highest Frequency: Dominant group across North, Central, and Western Indian states.
  • 🩸 High Hospital Consumption: Due to population prevalence, B+ blood units are continuously utilized in trauma units.
