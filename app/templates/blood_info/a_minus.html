Rare Blood Type Profile

Type A Negative (A-) Blood Group

A Negative is a rare blood type present in only ~6% of the population, making voluntary A- donations vital.

Transfusion Compatibility

Individuals with A- blood have A antigens on red blood cells but lack the Rh factor protein.

Can Donate Red Cells To:
A+ A- AB+ AB-
Can Receive Red Cells From:
A- O-

Donor Contribution Impact

Because A- can be transfused to both positive and negative A and AB patients, emergency rooms rely on A- inventory during surge demands.
