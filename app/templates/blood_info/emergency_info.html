Emergency Blood Request Protocols

Urgent hospital blood supply dispatch & emergency donor alert broadcast protocols.

🚨

1. Immediate Broadcast

When an emergency request is logged, instant alerts are dispatched to active voluntary donors registered within a 15km radius of the hospital.

🏥

2. Repository Reserve Lock

Emergency blood units are immediately reserved at the nearest certified repository for fast ambulance transit.

📞

3. Dedicated Hotline

Our 24/7 toll-free helpline 1800-RAKT-DAAN assists medical officers and patient families directly.
