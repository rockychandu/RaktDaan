Clinical Workflow

Detailed Voluntary Donation Process Guide

Step-by-step technical walkthrough of donor registration, medical screening, collection, and post-donation recovery.

Phase 1

1. Pre-Donation Screening

Verification of age (18-65), weight (>45kg), hemoglobin (>12.5 g/dL), and blood pressure.

Phase 2

2. Sterile Blood Collection

Collection of 350ml or 450ml whole blood using single-use sterile medical collection bags.

Phase 3

3. Testing & Storage

Pathogen screening (NAT/ELISA), component separation, and temperature-controlled refrigeration.
