Rarest Blood Type Profile

Type AB Negative (AB-) Blood Group

AB Negative is the rarest of all major blood groups, found in less than 1% of the population.

Transfusion Compatibility

AB- red blood cells carry both A and B antigens without the Rh protein factor.

Can Donate Red Cells To:
AB+ AB-
Can Receive Red Cells From:
AB- A- B- O-

Universal Plasma Donor

AB- plasma can be given to patients of any blood type! AB- plasma donors are invaluable assets to emergency trauma centers.
