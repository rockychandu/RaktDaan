Blood Group Profile

Type O Positive (O+) Blood Group

O Positive is the most common blood group in the world, present in over 38% of blood donors.

Transfusion Compatibility

O+ red cells carry no A or B antigens, but do carry the Rh factor protein.

Can Donate Red Cells To:
O+ A+ B+ AB+
Can Receive Red Cells From:
O+ O-

High Emergency Demand

Because O+ is the most common blood type, it is constantly in highest demand across trauma units, surgeries, and emergency transfusions.
