Quality & Safety Standards

Blood Safety & Testing Protocols

Rigorous 5-point screening protocols applied to every voluntary donation unit.

🔬

1. Pathogen Screening

Every unit undergoes mandatory nucleic acid testing (NAT) and ELISA screening for HIV 1&2, Hepatitis B & C, Syphilis, and Malaria.

🧪

2. ABO & Rh Typing

Dual-check blood group typing and antibody screening guarantee exact recipient compatibility matching.

❄️

3. Cold Chain Storage

Continuous temperature monitoring maintains red cells at 2°C–6°C and frozen plasma at -30°C with automated power backup.
