Blood Group Profile

Type A Positive (A+) Blood Group

A+ is one of the most common blood types in India, representing approximately 30% of the population.

Transfusion Compatibility

Individuals with A+ blood have A antigens on red blood cells and Rh factor protein present.

Can Donate Blood To:
A+ AB+
Can Receive Blood From:
A+ A- O+ O-

Key Facts & Importance

  • 🩸 High Demand: High prevalence in hospitals makes A+ constant in demand for surgeries and trauma recovery.
  • 🩸 Platelet Preference: A+ platelets are universal and can be transfused to patients of any blood type.
  • 🩸 Red Cell Storage: Collected red cells are stored at 2°C–6°C for up to 42 days.
