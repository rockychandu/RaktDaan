Universal Donor Profile

Type O Negative (O-) Blood Group

O Negative is the Universal Red Cell Donor type — essential for emergency trauma rooms when a patient's blood type is unknown.

Universal Red Cell Donor

O- red blood cells carry no A, B, or Rh antigens on the membrane, preventing immune rejection across all recipients.

Can Donate Blood To:
ALL TYPES (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-)
Can Receive Blood From:
O- ONLY

Emergency Critical Importance

  • 🚑 First Response: Used in ER trauma cases, newborn blood transfusions, and urgent surgeries before lab cross-matching.
  • ⚠️ Rarity: Only ~7% of the general population has O- blood, making voluntary O- donors highly sought after.
  • ❤️ Life-Saving Impact: Voluntary O- donations save critically injured patients every single day.
