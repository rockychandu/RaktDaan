Universal Recipient Profile

Type AB Positive (AB+) Blood Group

AB Positive individuals are Universal Red Cell Recipients — able to receive blood from any blood group type.

Transfusion Compatibility

AB+ carries both A and B antigens and Rh factor. Thus, antibodies against A, B, or Rh are absent in plasma.

Can Donate Blood To:
AB+ ONLY
Can Receive Blood From:
ALL TYPES (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-)

Universal Plasma Donor

While AB+ red cells can only be given to AB+ patients, AB+ plasma is Universal Plasma and can be safely transfused to patients of any blood group!
