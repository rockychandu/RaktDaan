Post-Donation Recovery

Post-Donation Care & Recovery Tips

How to care for yourself during the 24 hours following blood donation.

🥤

Hydrate & Snack

Enjoy complimentary juices and snacks in the donor rest lounge for 15 minutes.

🩹

Keep Bandage On

Keep the pressure bandage on your arm for at least 4 hours to ensure complete venipuncture healing.

🛌

Rest & Avoid Heavy Lifting

Avoid strenuous exercise or heavy lifting with your donation arm for 24 hours.
