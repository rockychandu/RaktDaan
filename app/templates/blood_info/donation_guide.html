Voluntary Donor Handbook & Guide

Everything you need to know about becoming a voluntary blood donor with RaktDaan.

📋

1. Registration & Consent

Complete your profile online or at the blood collection center. Fill out a simple confidential medical history questionnaire.

🩺

2. Mini-Physical Check

A qualified nurse checks your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin level via a quick finger-prick test.

🩸

3. Safe Donation

Relax on a comfortable reclining chair for 8-10 minutes while approximately 350ml-450ml of blood is collected using sterile equipment.
