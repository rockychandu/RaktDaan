Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers regarding voluntary blood donation eligibility, preparation, safety standards, and blood request processes.

Any healthy individual aged 18 to 65, weighing at least 45 kg, with a pulse rate between 60-100 beats/min and hemoglobin above 12.5 g/dL can voluntarily donate blood.
Healthy male donors can donate blood once every 3 months (90 days), while female donors can donate once every 4 months (120 days) to allow iron stores to replenish naturally.
Yes, 100% safe. Every blood donation is collected using a brand-new, sterile, single-use needle kit that is safely disposed of immediately after use. You cannot contract any infection from donating blood.
Hospitals or verified patient representatives submit an urgent request through our portal. The platform matches availability in real-time and dispatches units from the nearest repository.
Drink plenty of water, eat a healthy meal 2-3 hours prior to donation, and avoid strenuous exercise for 12 hours post-donation. Free refreshments are provided at all RaktDaan donation centers.
