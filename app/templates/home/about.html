About RaktDaan

Saving Lives Through Voluntary Donation

RaktDaan is an enterprise-grade voluntary Blood Bank Management System dedicated to closing the critical gap between blood donors, certified blood repositories, and urgent hospital patient needs.

🎯

Our Mission

To ensure every hospital, medical emergency unit, and patient has instant, transparent access to safe and verified voluntary blood units without delay.

🔒

Safety & Verification

All registered donors undergo strict age, medical eligibility, and health screening criteria before every donation cycle.

⚡

Emergency Response

Real-time blood stock tracking enables rapid emergency dispatch during critical surgeries, trauma cases, and rare blood group requirements.
