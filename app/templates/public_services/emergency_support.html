Emergency Blood Support & Dispatch

Round-the-clock emergency support for hospitals and acute medical trauma cases.

Urgent Request Protocol

When an emergency blood requirement is flagged, RaktDaan instantly locks available inventory units at the closest repository and dispatches SMS/Push alerts to registered donors.

Required Information for Requests

  • 📌 Patient Name & Hospital Reg ID
  • 📌 Required Blood Group & Component Type
  • 📌 Number of Units Required
  • 📌 Hospital Address & Treating Doctor Contact
