RaktDaan Help Center & Knowledge Base

Find immediate assistance for donor registration, login issues, and blood requests.

If you forgot your password, contact support or click the password assistance link on the login page. Ensure your registered email is active.
RaktDaan supports all 8 major blood groups (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-). For rare sub-types (e.g. Bombay Blood Group), contact our emergency hotline.
Hospitals and licensed medical units can submit requests via the Emergency Blood Request button or helpline 1800-RAKT-DAAN.
