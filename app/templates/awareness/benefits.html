Health Benefits of Blood Donation

Donating blood not only saves lives — it offers health benefits for donors too!

❤️

1. Heart Health Promotion

Regular blood donation reduces excess iron accumulation in the blood, promoting cardiovascular health and lowering heart attack risks.

🩺

2. Free Mini-Health Screening

Before every donation, your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels are evaluated free of charge.

🔄

3. Stimulates Blood Cell Production

Donating triggers your bone marrow to produce fresh, healthy red blood cells within days, refreshing your circulatory system.
