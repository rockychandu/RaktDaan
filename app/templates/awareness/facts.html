Essential Facts About Voluntary Donation

Key medical facts every voluntary donor should know.

🩸

1. One Donation = 3 Components

Whole blood is separated into Red Blood Cells, Fresh Frozen Plasma, and Platelets — saving up to 3 lives.

⏱️

2. Takes Only 10 Minutes

The actual blood collection takes only 8-10 minutes. The entire registration and checkup process takes ~30 minutes.

🔄

3. Rapid Plasma Replenishment

Your body replaces lost fluid volume (plasma) within 24 hours. Red blood cells rebuild completely within 4-8 weeks.
