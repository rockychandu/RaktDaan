Blood Donation Myths vs Medical Facts

Debunking common misconceptions about voluntary blood donation with clinical facts.

Myth 1

"Donating blood makes you weak or sick."

Medical Fact

Your body replaces plasma volume within 24 hours and red cells within 4-8 weeks. Normal physical activity can be resumed the same day!

Myth 2

"You can contract HIV or infections while donating."

Medical Fact

Blood donation is 100% safe. Every collection needle and bag is sterile, single-use, and discarded immediately after use.
